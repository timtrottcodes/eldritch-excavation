# ⚫ Eldritch Excavation ⚫

*"In the depths beyond reason, beyond time, treasures wait for those brave—or mad—enough to seek them."*

A Lovecraftian idle clicker game where ancient horrors become your workforce and reality itself becomes your mine.

---

## 📖 The Backstory

### **The Discovery**

In 1927, a failed archaeologist named Dr. Ambrose Blackwood stumbled upon something impossible in the mountains of Scotland—a crystal that shouldn't exist. It pulsed with impossible geometry, shifting between dimensions with each heartbeat. When he touched it, the visions began.

Deep beneath the earth, older than humanity, older than life itself, lay deposits of materials that defied natural law. Iron and copper from the dawn of time. Gold touched by dying stars. Gemstones that bent light in ways physics couldn't explain. And deeper still—minerals from beyond our reality, seeping through cracks in the fabric of space itself.

But mortal hands could never mine such depths. The pressure would crush bone. The darkness would drive minds to breaking. So Dr. Blackwood did what no sane person would: he called to the things that dwell in those spaces between worlds.

### **The Pact**

Through rituals found in the forbidden *Necronomicon* and whispered secrets from the void, Blackwood learned to summon them—the Cultists, the Ghouls, the Deep Ones. Creatures that thrived in darkness and madness. Entities that could dig where humans dare not go.

At first, they brought simple ores. Iron. Copper. Lead. But as Blackwood delved deeper, the minerals grew stranger. Silver that gleamed with moonlight even in perfect darkness. Diamonds that contained glimpses of other times. Moldavite born from cosmic impacts. And finally... the impossible materials.

**Voidstone** - matter from the space between spaces
**Star-Metal** - forged in the hearts of dying suns
**Time Crystals** - patterns that repeat in time, not space
**Reality Shards** - fragments of broken universes

### **The Madness**

But such power comes at a cost. Each excavation, each discovery, each communion with the entities beyond—it all extracts a toll on the mind. Sanity frays. Reality bends. The line between the possible and impossible blurs.

Blackwood learned to *embrace* the madness. To let go of rational thought and tap into cosmic power. Each time he reset his operations, purging his mind and starting anew, he returned *changed*—more powerful, more attuned to the eldritch frequencies that govern reality.

### **Your Role**

You have inherited Blackwood's journal. His crystal. His terrible knowledge. Now **you** must continue the excavation, calling forth horrors to mine the unmineble, sending expeditions into cursed ruins, and collecting artifacts that should not exist.

The question is not whether you will succeed—the materials are there, waiting.

The question is: **How much of your sanity are you willing to sacrifice for ultimate power?**

## ✨ Core Features

### 💎 **35 Dynamic SVG Crystals**
Each ore tier has a **unique, hand-crafted SVG crystal** that evolves in complexity:
- **Common Metals (1-7)**: Simple geometric shapes - hexagons, pentagons, stars
- **Precious Metals (8-14)**: Multi-layered faceted gems with radial gradients
- **Gemstones (15-21)**: Brilliant cuts with inner cores and light reflections
- **Rare Minerals (22-28)**: Complex geode formations with clustered crystals
- **Eldritch Materials (29-35)**: Animated void cores with pulsing energy, rotating layers, chaos effects

**Visual Evolution:**
- Iron: Simple brown hexagon → Azathoth's Core: 16 tendrils, 3 rotating rings, nuclear chaos
- Each tier changes the entire game's color theme
- Intelligent text contrast (dark text on light ores, light text on dark ores)

### ✨ **Progressive Particle Effects**
Click animations evolve with ore tiers:
- **Tier 1-2**: Dust and rubble (simple scatter)
- **Tier 3**: Small sparks with glow
- **Tier 4**: Rotating stars with triple glow layers
- **Tier 5**: Intense eldritch sparks + pulsating energy rings

### 🗺️ **Expedition System** *(NEW!)*
Timed missions to discover ancient relics:
- **6 Expedition Types**: 5 minutes to 6 hours
- **Dynamic Rewards**: Scale with your production and progress
- **Relic Discovery**: Hidden artifacts unlock through exploration, not purchase
- **One at a Time**: Strategic commitment - no recalls
- **Weighted Tiers**: Longer expeditions find rarer relics
- **Prestige Synergy**: +2% relic chance per prestige (max +20%)

### 🏆 **100+ Achievement System**
Seven categories of achievements with permanent bonuses:
- 👆 **Click Master** (20) - Clicking milestones
- 💎 **Ore Baron** (15) - Ore collection goals
- 🔨 **Tool Collector** (15) - Worker progression
- 🌟 **Prestige Hunter** (10) - Madness and prestige milestones
- 📦 **Collection Master** (15) - Upgrades and relics
- 🗺️ **Deep Miner** (10) - Ore tier exploration
- 🏃 **Speedrunner** (3) - Time-based challenges
- 🔍 **Secret Hunter** (8) - Hidden achievements

**Rewards:** Global multipliers, click power, production boosts, crit bonuses

### 💥 **Critical Hit System**
- 5% base crit chance (upgradeable to 25%+)
- 5x damage multiplier (upgradeable)
- **Visual Effects**: Golden damage numbers, screen flash, explosion rings
- Streak tracking for consecutive crits
- Achievements for 10+ crit streaks

### 🌌 **Ascension System** *(Second Prestige Layer)*
- **Unlocks at 10 Prestiges**
- Resets ALL Madness and Prestiges for **Cosmic Power**
- **Formula**: `sqrt(totalMadnessSpent) / 10`
- **20 Cosmic Upgrades** across 4 tiers (1 CP, 3 CP, 10 CP, 25 CP)
- **Permanent Bonuses**: Click power, production, madness gain, crit chance, tool efficiency
- **Tier 4 Ultimate Upgrades**: 100x multipliers, start at Silver Ore, Absolute Certainty (guaranteed crits)

### 👾 **25 Lovecraftian Workers**
Summon eldritch entities to do your bidding:
- **Level System**: 0-256 per worker
- **Rarity Tiers**: Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythical → Divine → Transcendent → Omega
- **Logarithmic rarity progression**: Thresholds become progressively harder, ending at Omega (Lv 255)
- **Progressive Unlock**: Hidden until previous worker reaches Epic
- **Progression**: Cultist → Ghoul → Shoggoth → Deep One → Mi-Go → ... → Cthulhu → Azathoth
- **Exponential Scaling**: Each tier massively stronger than the last

### 🔮 **25 Ancient Relics**
Powerful artifacts discovered through expeditions:
- **Hidden Until Found**: Progressive mystery reveal system
- **Discovery Only**: Cannot be purchased - must be earned
- **Permanent Power**: Persist through all prestiges and ascensions
- **Expedition Tiers**: Different expeditions find different rarity relics
- Examples: Eye of Azathoth, Necronomicon, Black Stone of R'lyeh, Shining Trapezohedron

### ⚡ **60+ Upgrade Trees**
Five distinct upgrade paths, hidden until unlocked:
- **Click Power**: 15 tiers (2x → 3x → 5x progression)
- **Production**: 15 tiers (passive ore generation)
- **Auto-Clickers**: 10 tiers (automatic clicking)
- **Tool Efficiency**: 10 tiers (worker boost)
- **Global Multipliers**: 10 tiers (everything at once)

### 🌟 **Dual Prestige System**
**Madness (Tier 1):**
- Reset progress for Madness currency
- 40+ Prestige Upgrades
- Permanent multipliers that affect all future runs
- Relics persist (never lose them!)

**Ascension (Tier 2):**
- Reset Madness itself for Cosmic Power
- Ultimate endgame meta-progression
- Reality-bending upgrades
- Start new runs with massive advantages

### 📜 **50 Missions**
Track your progress across seven categories:
- Clicking, Ore Collection, Worker Management
- Upgrades, Relics, Prestige, Ore Tiers
- Rewards: Bonus ore and madness

### 📱 **Mobile-Optimized UI**
- **Desktop**: Side panels with tab navigation
- **Mobile**: Bottom drawer with swipe gestures
- **Compact Stats Bar**: All key info always visible
- **Touch-Friendly**: Large buttons, smooth animations
- **Responsive**: Adapts to any screen size

### 💾 **Quality of Life**
- ✅ Auto-save every 30 seconds
- ✅ Manual save (Ctrl+S)
- ✅ Particle effects toggle
- ✅ Keyboard shortcuts
- ✅ No ads, no monetization, no tracking
- ✅ Pure client-side (no server needed)

## 🎯 How to Play - Complete Guide

### **🌙 Phase 1: The First Descent (0-30 minutes)**

**Starting Out:**
1. **Click the Crystal** - Each click extracts ore (starts at 1 ore/click)
2. **Watch the Crystal Transform** - As you unlock new ores, the SVG crystal evolves in real-time
3. **Buy Your First Worker** - Cultist Miner (costs 10 ore, produces 0.5/sec)
4. **Purchase Click Upgrades** - Double, triple, then 5x your click power

**Early Strategy:**
- Focus on clicking upgrades first (cheap, immediate power)
- Buy 10-20 levels of Cultist Miner
- Unlock Copper Ore (500 total ore)
- Complete early missions for bonus rewards

**Key Milestone:** Unlock your second worker (Ghoul) when Cultist reaches Level 100

---

### **⚡ Phase 2: Automation Awakens (30 min - 2 hours)**

**Building Your Workforce:**
1. **Level Workers to 100** - Each one unlocks the next
2. **Balance Your Team** - Don't max one worker, spread levels
3. **Watch Rarities** - Common → Uncommon (Lv 25) → Rare (Lv 50) → Epic (Lv 100)
4. **Buy Production Upgrades** - Boost all workers at once

**Expedition Introduction:**
- **First Expedition**: Try a 5-minute Quick Scout
- **Goal**: Discover your first relic
- **Strategy**: Run short expeditions frequently while actively playing

**Key Milestones:**
- Silver Ore unlocked (5M total ore)
- 3-4 workers unlocked
- First expedition completed
- 1-2 relics discovered

---

### **💎 Phase 3: Gemstone Glory (2-8 hours)**

**Mid-Game Power Spike:**
1. **Unlock Gemstones** - Garnet (250B), Amethyst (1T), Ruby (5T)
2. **Complete Upgrade Trees** - Finish all 60 upgrades
3. **Run 30-Minute Expeditions** - Better rewards, higher relic chance
4. **Discover 5-10 Relics** - Permanent power boosts

**Critical Hits Unlock:**
- 5% chance for 5x damage
- Golden damage numbers
- Screen flash + explosion rings
- Achievement hunting begins

**Key Milestone:** Reach 1M total ore - prepare for first prestige

---

### **🌟 Phase 4: Embracing Madness (First Prestige)**

**When to Prestige:**
- Formula: `sqrt(totalOre / 1M)` = Madness gained
- Recommended: 10+ Madness (requires ~100M total ore)
- **What You Lose**: All ore, all workers, all upgrades
- **What You Keep**: Relics, achievements, missions

**After Prestige:**
1. **Spend Madness** - Buy prestige upgrades (click mult, production mult)
2. **Rebuild Faster** - Madness gives 10% bonus per prestige
3. **Track Madness Spending** - Needed for Ascension later
4. **Repeat** - Each prestige should be faster than the last

**Prestige Strategy:**
- First 3 prestiges: Buy click multipliers
- Prestiges 4-10: Balance click and production
- Track total madness spent (shows in Ascension tab later)

---

### **🗺️ Phase 5: Expedition Mastery (10+ Prestiges)**

**Advanced Expeditions:**
- **Active Play**: 10-30 minute expeditions
- **Before Bed**: Launch 6-hour Grand Expedition
- **During Work**: 3-hour Perilous Journey
- **Relic Chance**: Base + (Prestiges × 2%) = up to 95%!

**Relic Collection:**
- **Goal**: Discover all 25 relics
- **Tier Matching**: Long expeditions find rare relics
- **Prestige Synergy**: More prestiges = better discovery rates
- **Celebration**: Epic golden popup when discovered

**Key Milestone:** 10 Prestiges = Ascension unlocks!

---

### **🌌 Phase 6: Ascension (Endgame)**

**The Second Layer:**
1. **Requirements**: 10+ Prestiges, significant Madness spent
2. **Cosmic Power Gained**: `sqrt(totalMadnessSpent) / 10`
3. **Reset**: Lose ALL Madness and Prestiges
4. **Keep**: Relics, Achievements, Cosmic Upgrades

**Cosmic Upgrade Tiers:**
- **Tier 1 (1 CP)**: Foundation - 2x multipliers
- **Tier 2 (3 CP)**: Enhancement - 5x multipliers, special abilities
- **Tier 3 (10 CP)**: Mastery - 10x multipliers, game-changers
- **Tier 4 (25 CP)**: Transcendence - 100x multipliers, reality-breaking

**Post-Ascension Strategy:**
1. Buy Tier 1 upgrades immediately (cheap, powerful)
2. Prestiges become 10-100x faster
3. Work toward Tier 4 ultimate upgrades
4. Complete all 100+ achievements
5. Discover all 25 relics
6. Master the void

## 🔧 Technical Details

### **Stack**
- **HTML5** - Structure and semantic markup
- **CSS3** - Animations, transitions, gradients, responsive design
- **JavaScript (ES6)** - Game logic and state management
- **jQuery** - DOM manipulation and event handling
- **SVG** - Dynamic crystal generation and visual effects

### **Architecture**
- **Pure Client-Side** - No server, no backend, no API calls
- **LocalStorage** - Save data persists in browser
- **Modular Design**:
  - `game-data.js` - Ore, worker, upgrade definitions
  - `achievements-data.js` - 100+ achievement definitions
  - `ascension-data.js` - Cosmic Power upgrade tree
  - `expedition-data.js` - Timed mission system
  - `crystal-svg.js` - 35 SVG crystal generators
  - `game.js` - Core game loop and logic

### **Performance**
- ✅ Hardware-accelerated animations
- ✅ Efficient game loop (100ms tick rate)
- ✅ Smart rendering (only update visible elements)
- ✅ Optimized for mobile devices
- ✅ No external dependencies beyond jQuery
