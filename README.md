# ⚫ Eldritch Excavation ⚫

A Lovecraft-themed idle clicker game inspired by Crystalia Idle Clicker.

## 🎮 Game Overview

Delve into the unknowable depths and excavate real-world minerals and gemstones, progressing from humble iron ore to the impossible voidstone. Summon eldritch entities to do your bidding, unlock cursed artifacts, and embrace the madness of prestige mechanics.

## ✨ Features

### 🪨 Real-World Mineral Progression
- 16 tiers of minerals from Iron to Voidstone
- Real metals, precious metals, and gemstones
- Each ore has unique descriptions and colors
- Realistic value scaling (no quadrillions!)

### 👾 Lovecraft-Themed Tools
- 10 automated workers inspired by H.P. Lovecraft's mythos:
  - Cultist Miners
  - Shoggoth Workers
  - Deep One Excavators
  - Night-gaunt Haulers
  - Byakhee Transports
  - Star-spawn Overseers
  - Elder God Fragments
  - Azathoth's Dream
  - Yog-Sothoth Gates
  - Cthulhu's Blessing

### ⚡ Progression Systems
- **Click Upgrades**: Multiply your clicking power
- **Production Upgrades**: Boost passive ore generation
- **Auto-Clickers**: Automatic clicking while you idle
- **Relics**: Powerful permanent artifacts with unique bonuses
- **Prestige System**: Reset for "Madness" currency and permanent upgrades

### 📜 Missions
- 14 unique missions with various requirements
- Rewards include ore and madness
- Track your achievements and progress

### 💾 Quality of Life
- Auto-save every 30 seconds
- Manual save option
- Settings for particles and effects
- Keyboard shortcuts (Space to click, Ctrl+S to save)
- Responsive design for different screen sizes
- Dark, atmospheric Lovecraft theme

## 🎯 How to Play

1. **Click the Crystal**: Click the central ore crystal to mine ore
2. **Buy Tools**: Purchase automated workers to generate ore passively
3. **Unlock Upgrades**: Increase your clicking power and production rates
4. **Discover Relics**: Find powerful artifacts for permanent bonuses
5. **Complete Missions**: Achieve goals for bonus rewards
6. **Prestige**: Reset your progress to gain Madness and unlock permanent upgrades

## 🎨 Lovecraft Theme

The game features a dark, cosmic horror aesthetic inspired by H.P. Lovecraft's works:

- Shadowy, mysterious interface
- Eldritch entity names and descriptions
- "Madness" as the prestige currency

## 🔧 Technical Details

- **HTML5** for structure
- **jQuery** for DOM manipulation
- **CSS3** for animations and styling
- **LocalStorage** for save/load functionality
- Pure client-side - no server required
- No advertisements or monetization

## 📊 Game Balance

The game uses realistic number scaling:
- Numbers grow from 1 to millions/billions
- No need for scientific notation or made-up units
- Balanced progression curve
- Prestige available after reaching 1M+ total ore
- Each prestige gives meaningful permanent bonuses

## 🌟 Tips for Players

- Early game: Focus on clicking upgrades and cheap tools
- Mid game: Balance tool purchases with production upgrades
- Late game: Invest in relics for multiplicative bonuses
- Always complete missions for bonus rewards
- Prestige when madness gain is meaningful (10+ recommended)
- Relics persist through prestige - they're very valuable!
